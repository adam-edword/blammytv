## What to look for when you're back

- **Live TV → Guide:** the capsule in the ruler's top-left corner. Later
  moves a screen; around 14 presses reach the end of the day, past a
  "Sun 12:00 AM" tick. Scrolled ahead at a half-hour mark, the programme
  you're on shouldn't jump.
- **Settings → Sources:** "Guides refresh every 12 hours. Last
  refreshed …" and Refresh now. A relaunch inside 12 hours shouldn't
  download the guide (the console logs "reusing its guide").
- **Guide → right-click a channel with "No Information" → Fix guide…:**
  the closest names come first; Use this guide, and the lane fills after
  the guide downloads again (about a minute on your line). It shows up
  after your guide's next download, since older saved guides didn't keep
  the list (or press Refresh now in Settings).
- **Settings (the gear):** five pages down the left, each with a line
  under its name. It opens on the page you left. Playback has the four
  rows that were under Customize → Stream (Preferred Language and Skip
  Behavior now stack their controls under the label, the column being
  narrower). Appearance → Reset takes two presses. Squeeze the window
  under about 740px and the rail turns into a row of five names.
- **Ctrl+K, "subtitle", Enter:** Settings on Playback, Preferred
  Language lit for a second and a half, the audio picker focused. "row
  size" scrolls Appearance down to Catalog Row Size; "channel numbers"
  opens Appearance on its Live TV pill. The gear still opens Settings
  where you left it, nothing lit.
- **Settings → Playback → Skipping:** set Intro to Automatic and play an
  anime episode (AniSkip timings) or a show with intro markers: it jumps
  past the intro and the chip's spot says "Skipped Intro"; click it and you
  are back at the intro's start, and it plays through this time. Your old
  Skip Behavior carried over (Hidden is every type Off, Combine is Combine
  on). Autoplay Off: the end card waits with no countdown.
- **After the rebuild, Settings → Playback → Player:** Audio Channels on
  5.1 or 7.1 means "up to": a stereo film stays stereo (press `i`, the
  Channels row). Subtitle Size on Large, then a film with subtitles. Pop-out
  on Small, bottom right, then pop something out: it should sit above the
  taskbar, 24px in. In the player with subtitles on, `x` twice reads
  "Subtitles +0.2s" at the top; the next film starts at 0.
- **Any VOD with 5.1:** press `i`, read Channels.
- **Should look identical:** everything else, multi-view included. The CSS
  rewrite touches no rule that matches anything on screen today.
- **Not checkable from here:** WebView2's text rendering on the guide's
  title fades, your real 95MB guide, surround on your setup.
