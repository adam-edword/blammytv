# Plan 036: The mini-guide

Status: MOCK APPROVED 2026-11-03 (https://claude.ai/artifact/Rp3rFRxDmK1P6fQqe9VrPt), building.

On the roadmap since 1.0 ("Timeshift, the mini-guide strip"). Live TV in
theater or fullscreen is a dead end today: the player has no way to change
channel, so you leave it, find the channel in the Guide and come back.

## Decided (Adam, 2026-11-03)

- **Two shapes, with a toggle:** a bottom strip (channel cards: logo, what's
  on, progress) and a side list (channels with now and next down the left).
  Adam: "Both? With a toggle?"
- **↑/↓ stay volume.** PageUp/PageDown change channel straight away. G opens
  and closes the mini-guide.
- **The channels are the Guide's list:** the folder or Favorites you were
  browsing when you pressed play, in the Guide's order.

## Calls made here (Adam can overrule)

- **The toggle lives in the mini-guide** (one button in its corner that
  flips Strip and List) and the choice is remembered. Not a setting too.
- **Live only, theater and fullscreen.** Not the mini player, not films and
  shows, not Multi-View.
- **Browsing doesn't tune.** Inside the mini-guide the arrows move and Enter
  or a click tunes. PageUp/PageDown tune at once and show the strip for a
  moment so you can see where you landed.
- **A button in the player's controls opens it**, so it's findable without
  the key.
- **No last-channel key** this round. It's cheap and could follow.

## Open until the mock

- Card size and how many show; where the toggle sits; how now/next and the
  progress read on each shape.
