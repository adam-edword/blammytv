**Library** is the third view on the Stream rail. It holds two things:
what you are part-way through, and collections you have made yourself.

## Continue watching

A row of what you have started and not finished, most recent first. Clicking a
card resumes it where you left off.

Each card also gives you:

- **Sources** — jump straight to that title's source list, without opening the
  detail page first. Clicking the card's **title** does the same thing.
- **Remove** — take one entry off the row.

The row obeys your row-size setting, so it is not the whole history. The
built-in **Library** card opens the uncapped view: everything you have ever
started, however long the list is.

## Your lists

Named collections, as many as you like. Each holds card-shaped snapshots, so a
list paints instantly without going back to the network.

You can:

- **Create** and **rename** lists
- **Add** a title from its detail page, with the save button
- **Set a cover**, or let the list derive art from its first entry
- **Delete** a list, or remove single entries

Lists are ordered by when you created them.

## Where this lives

On your machine, in the app's local storage, alongside the rest of your
settings. Nothing is synced anywhere, which means two things worth knowing:
your history is private, and it does not follow you to another computer.

Clearing the app's data clears this too. See [Where your data
goes](/how-it-works/).
