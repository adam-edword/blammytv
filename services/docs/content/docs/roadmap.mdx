Here's what's coming to BlammyTV. There aren't any dates: things ship when
they're ready, and the order might change.

## Next

- **More themes.** The four themes so far change colors. Fonts, corners and
  effects are still to come.

## Later

- **Android TV and Fire TV.** Watch on your TV and use your remote.

## TBD

- **Mac, Apple TV, iPhone and iPad.** If enough people ask, these will come!
  Let me know in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/mC2VWvcAJz) if this is something you
  would genuinely use.
