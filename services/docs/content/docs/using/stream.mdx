The **Stream** tab is movies and shows, served by your own
[AIOStreams](/sources/aiostreams/) instance, your own [Jellyfin](/sources/jellyfin/)
server, your IPTV provider's [Xtream](/sources/xtream/#movies-and-shows)
panel, or any mix of them. It has three pages in the nav bar: **Stream**,
**Discover** and **Library**. The page behind the logo is [Home](/using/home/),
the same rows mixed with your live ones.

## The Stream Page

Rows of posters, one per browsable catalog your addon exposes, with a featured
hero at the top.

- **Titles per Row** (Settings → Appearance) controls how many titles each row holds. 40
  by default.
- **Carousel Catalogs** (Settings → Appearance) pins which catalogs the hero draws from. Leave it
  empty for the default mix.
- Rows load independently, so one failing catalog goes missing rather than
  taking the page down with it.
- Your provider's folders are rows too, the ones you pick, in the order you
  set. See [Movies and shows](/sources/xtream/#movies-and-shows) for picking
  them.

## Discover

Search across your catalogs or browse by genre. The cards work the same as on
the Stream page. Use this when you know what you want or want to see what's
there.

The field in the nav bar searches while Discover is open. It says what it's
searching, like **Search Movies & Series…**. The **Any**, **Movies** and
**Series** buttons beside it narrow the search and the genre pages. Press
<kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> from anywhere to jump to it.
That works unless you're typing in a field or a player is up.

### Genres

**By Genre** is a row of genre cards at the top. Click one and Discover opens
that genre as rows, top to bottom. Comedy looks like this:

- **Popular Comedy**, marked **From TMDB**, is TMDB's most popular titles in
  the genre that one of your catalogs has. It needs six or more matches and it
  isn't shown under **Series**. A build made without a TMDB key never shows it.
- **Just Comedy** is titles tagged Comedy and nothing else.
- **Comedy Series** is the genre's shows. It shows under **Any**.
- **Mixes** are the rest, like **Romantic Comedy** or **Action Comedy**. A title
  is in one mix only. A mix needs six titles to get a row and there are at most
  six.

Each row shows its first 20 titles. **See All** on a row opens all of it as a
grid with a Back button. **See All Comedy** at the foot opens the whole genre.

Titles that aren't the genre are left out. The rest are ranked by how much of
the title is the genre. A title that leads with the genre comes first. Ted
Lasso is above Pulp Fiction on Comedy. After that, titles that more of your
catalogs list come first. Then the ones nearer the top of their catalogs.
Format tags like Reality or Talk Show don't make rows.

If a genre's titles say too little about their genres for rows to mean
anything, the page is one ranked grid instead.

### Ctrl+K Finds Titles Too

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> searches the whole app from anywhere: channels,
programmes on later, Settings and the places you can go. With a stream source
it finds films and shows too.

- Titles your catalogs have already loaded and titles in your lists show under
  **Films and Series**.
- Type two letters or more and it also searches the way Discover does. Up to
  five more titles show under **More Films and Series**, below everything else
  that matched. **Searching…** shows until the answer lands. If your sources
  don't answer it says **Couldn't search your sources. Try again in a moment.**
- Once you've typed two letters, the last line there is always **Search
  Discover for** what you typed. It opens Discover with the same words in its
  search field.

The round search button beside Settings opens the same thing.

## Opening a Title

Clicking a card opens its detail page: artwork, synopsis, and the list of
**sources** the addon resolved for it. Pick one to play.

**One-Click Play Movies** (Settings, off by default) changes this for *movies*. A
click starts the best already-resolved source immediately instead of opening
the page. Series always open their page, because there's no single obvious
thing to play. If nothing is resolved yet, the click falls back to the detail
page rather than appearing to do nothing.

The **Sources** button is also on Continue Watching cards, and on the card's
title, so you can jump back into the source list for something you're
part-way through without opening it first.

## The Player

Same player as [Live TV](/using/live-tv/), and the [same keyboard
shortcuts](/using/live-tv/#keyboard-shortcuts), with two differences for VOD:

- **A scrubber** to seek with.
- **`Escape` toggles theater and fullscreen** rather than stepping down and
  out. The ✕ is the only way out of the player. Live TV keeps the ladder, VOD
  doesn't, so a stray keypress can't dump you out of a film.

### Continuity Between Episodes

Your last **explicit** choices carry to the next thing you play: audio
language, subtitle language, playback speed. They're matched by language, not
by track number, because track numbering is per-file and means nothing across
episodes.

Only choices you make count. An episode that defaults to a different track
doesn't become your new preference.

Volume and mute are separate. Those apply to every playback, live included.

## Pop Out

The **Pop Out** button at the top of the player moves a film or an episode out
of the app. Where it goes is **Settings → Playback → Player → Pop Out Films and
Shows In**:

- **MPV** is BlammyTV's own player, in a floating window of its own. It's the
  default. Size and corner come from **Pop-Out Window** just below. You can
  still drag and resize the window once it's open. The app's player shows
  **Player popped out.** with a **Bring It Back** button. Close the window or
  press Bring It Back and playback returns in the app where the window got to.
- **VLC** opens the film or episode in VLC from your position and closes
  BlammyTV's player. The title's page is back and **Playing in VLC** shows for a
  few seconds. VLC's window is VLC's own. The size and corner settings don't
  apply.

With VLC, BlammyTV follows your position. It goes to Continue Watching and to
Trakt, AIOStreams and Jellyfin as it does in the app. The title counts as
watched at the same 90% line. Skipping and the Up Next card aren't there,
because those belong to BlammyTV's player.

If VLC isn't installed you'll see **VLC isn't installed.** with a **Download
VLC** button. The film keeps playing in BlammyTV. A VLC that won't start says
**Couldn't open VLC** and why.

Live TV always pops out in MPV, whatever this is set to. Starting something new
in VLC replaces the title BlammyTV is following.

## When a Source Dies Mid-Play

By default nothing happens automatically. The card offers **Try the Next
Source** and waits for you.

Turn on **Switch Sources When One Fails** in Settings if you'd rather it move on by
itself. It's off by default on purpose: an automatic jump burns through your
resolved sources without asking, which is wrong when a stream stalls for a
moment and would have recovered.
