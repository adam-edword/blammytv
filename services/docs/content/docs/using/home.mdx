Click the BlammyTV logo in the middle of the nav bar to open **Home**. It's one
page of rows: films and shows from your sources, live channels, games and
recordings. You pick the rows and their order in **Settings → Home**.

The logo glows while you're on Home and no tab is lit. Click it again to jump
back to the top. Without a live TV playlist the logo sits at the left end of
the bar. It's always there.

<Callout title="Stream Is Unchanged">

The [Stream](/using/stream/) tab keeps its own fixed order. Home is the mix:
the same films and shows plus your live rows, arranged the way you like. Until
you change anything, Home is Stream's page with the live rows added.

</Callout>

## The Rows

Home draws a row only when it's switched on and has something in it. A row
with nothing to show leaves no empty heading.

- **Featured Carousel** is the sliding hero at the top. It follows **Carousel
  Style** and the catalogs you picked in [Appearance](/using/appearance/#stream).
  It only has a switch.
- **Continue Watching** is what you've started and not finished. A card
  resumes it where you left off.
- **Up Next** is the next episode of the shows you're watching, from
  AIOStreams and Jellyfin. On the page the rows read Next Up. With both
  signed in, Jellyfin's says **on Jellyfin**.
- **Catalog rows** are the rows Stream shows: each AIOStreams catalog, each
  Jellyfin library and the folders you picked from an
  [Xtream playlist](/sources/xtream/#movies-and-shows). A card opens the
  title's page.
- **On Now** is your starred channels, each with what's on, a progress bar
  and what's next. A card plays the channel in the Guide's player.
- **Recent Channels** is the channels you tuned lately, on the same cards.
- **Today's Games** is today's games for the leagues, teams and conferences
  you follow in [Sports](/using/sports/). It obeys Sports' **Ranked** switch.
  A card opens the game in Sports' theater. Follow nothing in Sports and the
  row stays empty. Home doesn't ask for every league. Add a row per sport too,
  like **Basketball Today**, to give each sport its own place on Home. Every
  games row shares one read of the scores.
- **Recordings** is your 20 newest finished [recordings](/using/recording/).
  **Play** opens one in Live TV's player.
- **Saved** is one of your [Library](/using/library/#your-lists) lists as a
  row. Add as many lists as you like. A card opens the title's page.
- **Trending Movies**, **Trending Shows**, **Popular Movies** and **Popular
  Shows** are Trakt's own lists. They need Trakt connected in **Settings →
  Accounts** and hold as many titles as **Titles per Row** allows. Trakt
  doesn't send artwork. These cards show a letter where the poster would be.
- **Needs a Source** is a row imported from Strand that Home can't fill yet.
  See [Import Layout](#import-layout).

A title opened from Home opens right there. Back returns you to Home.

If every row is off or empty, Home says **Nothing on Home** with an **Edit
Home** button that opens Settings → Home. While a live row is still loading it
says **Loading Home…** instead.

### The First Time

The first time you open Home it sets itself up with the carousel (on if
**Featured Carousel** is on in Appearance), Continue Watching, Up Next, then
every catalog row in Stream's order. With a live TV playlist it adds On Now and
Today's Games as well. Everything else you add yourself.

## Settings → Home

**Settings → Home** is the first page in Settings. Every change applies as you
make it and Home moves behind Settings while you work. There's no Save button.

Each row in **Home Rows** has a small picture of its shape beside its name:
wide cards for Continue Watching, posters for a catalog, channel tiles for On
Now and so on. A row that's switched off shows its picture dimmed.

- **Reorder** by dragging a row by its handle. The keyboard works too. The
  carousel stays pinned to the top and has no handle.
- **Rename** a row by typing in its name. Clear the field and click away to
  get the row's own name back.
- **Switch** a row on or off. Off rows stay in the list.
- **Remove** a row with the ✕. Only the live rows, Saved, Trakt and Needs a
  Source rows have one.

Continue Watching, Up Next and the catalog rows can be switched off but not
removed. They come from your sources. A removed one would only come back at
the next launch. When a source gains a catalog, its row is added at the end
and switched on.

On Now and Today's Games are added once on their own when you first have a
playlist. If you take one off, it stays off.

### Add Row

**Add Row** offers the rows that aren't on Home yet, in groups:

- **Watching** has **Saved**, which opens a list of your Library lists.
- **Live TV** has **On Now**, **Recent Channels** and **Recordings**. On Now
  and Recent Channels need a live TV playlist. Recordings doesn't.
- **Sports** has **Today's Games** and one row for each sport you follow, like
  **Basketball Today**.
- **Films & Shows** has Trakt's four lists.

A group with nothing left to add isn't shown. **Add Row** goes away when every
row is on Home.

### Why a Row Is Empty

When a row has nothing to draw, a grey line under its name says why:

- **Star channels in the Guide to fill this row.** for On Now
- **Channels you watch show here.** for Recent Channels
- **Follow a league in Sports to fill this row.** or **No games today for your
  leagues.** for Today's Games, and **No games today.** for a sport's row
- **Recordings you make show here.** for Recordings
- **Save titles to this list to fill this row.** or **This list is gone.** for
  Saved
- **Sign in to Trakt to fill this row.** for a Trakt row
- **Add a playlist to fill this row.** for a live row with no playlist

### Import Layout

**Import Layout** brings in a layout exported from Strand. Paste it into the
box and press **Add Rows**. A line says how many rows were added and how many
were already on Home. If the text isn't a Strand layout you'll see **This isn't
a layout exported from Strand.** and nothing changes.

Imported rows go after the ones you have. They keep Strand's order, names and
switches.

- **Continue Watching** is skipped, because Home already has one. If Strand has
  it off, yours switches off.
- **Watchlist** becomes a Saved row on your Trakt Watchlist list when you have
  one. Otherwise it uses your first Library list. With no list at all it
  becomes a Needs a Source row.
- **Trending and popular lists** become the matching Trakt rows.
- **Anything else** becomes a **Needs a Source** row. It keeps Strand's row, so
  a later version can fill it. On Home it shows its name and **This row needs a
  source.**

Importing the same layout twice adds nothing the second time. A row Strand has
switched off is switched off here too. A row Strand has on never turns one of
yours back on.

## Open on Home

To start BlammyTV on Home, pick it under **Settings → Appearance → Startup
Tab**. Home is the first choice there. Like Stream and Discover it shows up
once you have something to stream: AIOStreams, Jellyfin or an Xtream playlist
with movies and shows. The default stays Live TV.
