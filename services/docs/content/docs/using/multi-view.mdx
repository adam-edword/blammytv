---
title: Multi-View
description: Watch up to four live channels at once, with the sound on the one that matters.
---

**Multi-View** plays up to four live channels side by side. It's a tab of its
own, between Guide and Sports. Any live channel can go in a tile, not just a
game.

Every tile is a stream from your provider, so your line's connection limit
decides how many you can open. See [When Your Line Is Full](#when-your-line-is-full).

## Add a Channel

There are three ways in:

- **From the Guide.** Right-click a channel and choose **Add to Multi-View**.
  You land in Multi-View with the channel in the grid and the sound on it.
- **From a player.** The **Watch in Multi-View** button in the Live TV player
  and the Sports player sends what you're watching. From Sports, a game goes
  in as a game, so its tile shows the score.
- **From Multi-View.** Press **Add Channel** on the bar, or press
  <kbd>A</kbd>. An empty grid has one big **Add a Channel** tile you can click
  instead.

A channel joins the grid you left. Nothing in it goes away unless you pick the
tile it replaces.

### The Picker

The picker is a search box over your channels.

- Type a name or a channel number to search every channel.
- Before you type, it lists **Live Games** (games on now that one of your
  channels carries), then your **Favorites** and **Recent** channels.
- Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click to add. A channel already in the grid says
  **In the Grid**.
- A game lists every channel of yours that carries it, with the best match
  marked **Best Match**. Pick the one you want. <kbd>Esc</kbd>,
  <kbd>Backspace</kbd> on an empty search, or the back arrow returns to the
  list.

## Layouts

The **Layout** switch on the left of the bar has two choices. It shows up once
you have two channels. With one, the channel fills the window.

- **Grid** gives every tile the same size. Two sit side by side, three go two
  over one, and four make a square.
- **Focus** makes one tile big and stacks the others beside it. The big tile
  is the one with the sound.

<kbd>G</kbd> switches between them. Focus is what you get until you pick
otherwise, and your pick is remembered for each number of tiles.

In Focus, drag the gap between the big tile and the rest to resize it. The
keys <kbd>{"["}</kbd> and <kbd>{"]"}</kbd> nudge it, and <kbd>{"\\"}</kbd> or a
double-click on the gap puts it back. Each number of tiles remembers its own
size.

Every picture is 16:9, with the channel's name and what's on under it. The
grid is still there the next time you open the tab.

### Fill the Window

Double-click a tile, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, and it takes the whole window.
The others keep playing out of sight. <kbd>Esc</kbd> or another double-click
puts it back.

The filled tile has the sound, so <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd> and the arrow
keys flip the whole window between channels.

## Sound

One tile has sound at a time. A speaker icon next to its name marks it, and
its badge says **Sound**, or **Muted** when the sound is off. To move it:

- Click a tile, or use the **Sound Here** button that shows when you hover one.
- Press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd> for that tile, or <kbd>←</kbd> and
  <kbd>→</kbd> to step through them.

In Focus, the tile with the sound is always the big one. Choosing a small tile
swaps it with the big one.

Volume and mute are on the right of the bar, and they apply to the tile with
the sound. The mouse wheel over that tile changes the volume too. Both are
remembered the next time you open the tab.

## Change or Close a Tile

Move the pointer over a tile to see its buttons:

- **Watch in Player** hands the channel to the Guide's player. See [Watch in
  Player](#watch-in-player).
- **Replace** opens the picker and swaps the tile in place. It keeps the sound.
  <kbd>R</kbd> does the same for the tile with the sound.
- **Close** removes the tile. <kbd>Delete</kbd> closes the one with the sound.

The bar and the tiles' buttons fade after two seconds without the pointer, and
come back when you move it.

## Live Scores

The score row runs along the bottom of the tab. Press <kbd>S</kbd>, or the
**Live Scores** button on the bar, to show or hide it. It's off until you turn
it on, and it stays how you left it.

- It lists live games only, grouped by league. Scroll it with the mouse wheel
  or drag it.
- Click a game one of your channels carries and the picker opens on that
  game's channels.
- A game none of your channels carry still shows its score. It does nothing
  when you click it.
- A game that's already in the grid is marked.
- **Choose Sports**, at the start of the row, opens **Scores Row**. It has a
  switch for each sport, and one for each league in a sport that has several.
  Changes apply at once. It offers the leagues you follow in
  [Sports](/using/sports/), or all of them if you follow none.

### Fill the Grid with Live Games

With the picker open and the search empty, **Fill with Live Games** adds as
many live games as your line has room for. Games with your followed teams go
first, then your followed leagues, then the rest. Each game goes in on its
best-matching channel. To change it, use **Replace** on the tile.

It only adds. It never replaces a tile you chose, and it doesn't show up when
there's nothing to add.

A game's tile shows its score. About half an hour after the game ends, the tile
goes back to being a plain channel.

### Add RedZone

When two or more NFL games are on at once and one of your channels is RedZone,
the NFL's games in the score row start with an **Add RedZone** button. It puts
the RedZone channel in the grid. If it's already there, the button is off.

If the NFL is switched off in **Choose Sports**, the button goes with it.

BlammyTV finds a channel named RedZone on its own. If it picks the wrong one,
right-click the right channel in the Guide and choose **Use for RedZone**. See
[Live TV](/using/live-tv/#right-click-a-channel).

## When Your Line Is Full

The meter on the left of the bar shows how much of your line is in use, like
**2 of 3 Streams in Use**. A count after a dot, like **1 Elsewhere**, is
streams open on other devices or apps. A provider with no limit shows only how
many streams the grid has.

- **Four is the most**, whatever your line allows.
- **Add Channel** turns off when there's no room. Hover it to see why.
- **A full line asks which tile to replace.** The bar says **Pick a Tile**, and
  every tile you can replace says **Swap for** the new channel. Click one, or
  press its number. **Cancel**, or <kbd>Esc</kbd>, lets it go.
- **A line that allows one stream can't run Multi-View.** The tab says so
  instead of showing a grid.
- **With more than one playlist, each channel is held to its own line.** The
  picker marks a channel that would replace a tile with **Replaces a Tile**.

A tile that loses its stream reconnects on its own. It waits for a free slot on
your line first. If your provider refuses a stream on a full line, the tile
says your line is at its limit and offers **Retry**.

Leaving the tab stops every tile and hands the connections back, so the Guide
or the player you go to next has the line to itself.

To see where the numbers come from, and why they can lag, read [Connection
limits](/troubleshooting/connections/).

## Watch in Player

**Watch in Player** on a tile sends that channel to the Guide's player, with
all of its controls. Multi-View stops while you're there, and your grid is
waiting when you come back.

A tile that can't play a channel's format says so, and offers **Watch in
Player** right on the tile.

## What Plays Differently

Multi-View doesn't use the main player. Its tiles play inside the app's own
window, and a notice says so the first time you open it. Press **Got It** to
carry on.

- HEVC channels are converted first, so they take a moment longer to start.
- Some audio formats can come up silent.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

These work in Multi-View. They don't work while you're typing in a field or
while a dialog is open.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>A</kbd> | Add a channel |
| <kbd>M</kbd> | Mute |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd> | Volume up / down, 5% a step. <kbd>↑</kbd> also unmutes |
| <kbd>G</kbd> | Switch between Grid and Focus, with two or more channels |
| <kbd>F</kbd> | Full screen |
| <kbd>S</kbd> | Show or hide Live Scores |
| <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd> | Put the sound on that tile. In Focus, it becomes the big tile |
| <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> | Move the sound to the previous / next tile |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | On a tile you've tabbed to, take the sound |
| <kbd>R</kbd> | Replace the tile with the sound |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> | Close the tile with the sound |
| <kbd>{"["}</kbd> / <kbd>{"]"}</kbd> | In Focus, make the big tile smaller / bigger |
| <kbd>{"\\"}</kbd> | In Focus, put the big tile back where it started |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Fill the window with the tile that has the keyboard, or the sound |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Step back one layer: the picker or a pending pick, then a filled tile, then full screen |

The same list is in **Settings → App → Keyboard Shortcuts**.
