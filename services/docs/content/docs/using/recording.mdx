---
title: Recording
description: Record a channel or a programme to your PC and play it back later. Pause or rewind live TV.
---

BlammyTV can record a live channel to a file on your PC. A recording is a copy
of the stream your provider sends. It plays later from **Live TV → Recordings**
or from [Library](/using/library/) and it stays after the channel moves on.

Recording is part of the Windows app. It needs a live TV [playlist](/sources/).

## Record What You're Watching

Press **Record** in the player's controls while a channel plays. The button
turns into **Stop Recording** with a red dot and a clock counting how long it's
been running. A red dot also marks the channel's card in the Guide.

A recording runs on its own. Change channel, jump to live or close the player
and it keeps going. Press **Stop Recording** to end it. It's there whenever you
come back to that channel and on the recording's row in the Recordings list.

- **Dropped streams.** If your provider drops the stream, BlammyTV reconnects
  into the same file for up to two minutes. After that the recording ends as
  **Partial** with **Stream lost.** as the reason.
- **Quitting BlammyTV** ends every recording and keeps the file. At the next
  launch it reads **Partial** with **App closed while recording.**
- **Windows won't go to sleep** while a recording runs.
- **Install and Restart wait.** An update won't install while a recording runs
  or is being finished.

### Disk Space

A recording won't start with under 2 GB free on the drive. Record says
**Couldn't record: not enough free disk space (recording needs 2 GB).** and
points you to **Settings → Playback**. A recording that's running stops when
the drive falls under 1 GB free. The file is kept and reads **Partial** with
**Disk full.**

## The Connection Warning

A recording is a stream of its own. It uses one of the connections your
provider allows for as long as it runs. The first time you press **Record** in
the player, a dialog says so:

- **Recording Uses a Connection** names your line's limit ("This recording uses
  one of the 3 streams your line allows, for as long as it runs."). A playlist
  that reports no limit says it uses one of your provider's streams.
- Tick **Don't Show Again** and press **Start Recording** and it won't ask
  again. **Cancel** backs out.

Multi-View counts your recordings on the line. Its grid keeps inside what's
left. See [Connection Limits](/troubleshooting/connections/).

### A Line With One Stream

If your line allows one stream there's no connection to spare. Pressing Record
on the channel you're watching shows **Record What You're Watching** instead.
It records from the picture on your screen rather than opening a second
stream. The dialog says what that costs: the recording stops if you change
channel, go live or the stream reconnects. What it had by then is kept as
**Partial**. If you switched channel it says **Stopped when the channel
changed.**

This has its own **Don't Show Again**.

### Bring the Warnings Back

If you ticked **Don't Show Again** on either dialog, **Settings → Playback →
Recordings** gets a **Show Recording Warnings Again** button. It turns both
back on. The button only shows while one of them is off.

## Record a Programme From the Guide

Right-click a programme in the Guide and pick **Record**. The Guide's toast says
what happened.

- **A programme that's on** starts recording straight away.
- **One that's coming** is scheduled. It starts a minute before it begins.
- **Both stop a minute after the programme ends** in case the guide runs late.

A scheduled programme has a hollow red ring in its cell in the Guide and a row
in the Recordings list that reads **Scheduled**. Programmes that are already
over don't offer Record. There are no series rules yet. Every programme is its
own recording.

### Scheduled Recordings

Scheduled recordings are kept across restarts but only run while BlammyTV is
open. If a start goes by while BlammyTV is closed or asleep, the recording shows
as **Missed** with **The app was not running at the start.** It's never started
late.

Two programmes back to back on a one-stream line both work. The first stops at
its programme's end so the second can start.

### Too Many at Once

BlammyTV counts your own recordings against your line's limit when you schedule
one. If there's no room it says so right away: **Couldn't schedule: 1 other
recording overlaps this one, and your line allows 1 stream at once.** The same
check runs for a programme that's on, as **Couldn't record: …**

It only counts recordings made in BlammyTV. If you're watching on the same line
or another device is using it, your provider decides. A line that reports no
limit (M3U, Stalker or a panel that doesn't say) is never turned down up front.
The attempt shows what the provider thinks.

### Cancel a Recording

Right-click the programme again and pick **Cancel Recording**. Or press
**Cancel Recording** on its row in the Recordings list. Either one removes the
schedule.

## Where Recordings Go

**Settings → Playback → Recordings → Recordings Folder** shows where new
recordings are saved. Until you change it, that's `Videos\BlammyTV`. **Change…**
opens a folder picker.

A new folder applies to recordings from then on. Ones you've already made stay
where they were.

Files are named with the channel, the date and time and the programme when
there is one, like `Channel 2026-11-02 2000 - Programme.mkv`. Long names are
shortened.

## The Recordings List

The Recordings list is in two places. It's the same list:

- **Live TV → Recordings**, the fourth mode in the sidebar beside Playlist,
  Favorites and Recents.
- **Library → Recordings**, a section at the bottom once you've made one.

Newest first. Each row shows the programme or channel name, the channel and
when, the status, the length and the size. Home can show them too. See
[Home](/using/home/).

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Recording** | Running now |
| **Scheduled** | Waiting for its programme. **Starting** just before it begins |
| **Done** | Ran to the end |
| **Partial** | Ended early with what it had kept. A reason follows: **Stream lost.**, **App closed while recording.**, **Disk full.** or **Stopped when the channel changed.** |
| **Failed** | Nothing was recorded or a scheduled one couldn't start. A reason follows |
| **Missed** | BlammyTV wasn't running at the start |

A row has these buttons:

- **Play** opens it in Live TV's player. From Library it takes you to the
  Guide.
- **Show in Folder** opens the file's folder.
- **Delete** asks twice. Press it again at **Click Again to Confirm**.
- **Stop Recording** or **Cancel Recording** replaces Delete while a recording
  runs or waits.

With nothing recorded the list says **No Recordings Yet**.

## Finishing a Recording

While it records, a recording is a `.ts` file, because that survives a crash or
a power cut. When it ends, BlammyTV repackages it into an `.mkv` without
re-encoding. The row says **Waiting to Finish** and then **Finishing 40%**.
The `.mkv` plays in more apps and seeks better.

The `.ts` is only replaced once the `.mkv` plays through to the end at the
same length. If it doesn't, the `.ts` stays and the row says why. Older `.ts`
recordings are repackaged one at a time the first time you launch a version
that does this.

### Smaller Files

**Settings → Playback → Recordings → Recording Quality** has two choices:

- **Original Quality** keeps each recording as it was recorded. It's the
  default.
- **Smaller Files** compresses each recording after it finishes, using HEVC.
  Audio is copied as it is. BlammyTV uses your graphics card's encoder when
  there is one (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) and the processor otherwise. Channels
  that are interlaced are cleaned up on the way.

The row reads **Waiting to Shrink** and then **Shrinking 70%**. When it's done
it reads how much it saved, like **Saved 1.2 GB**. It applies to recordings
that end after you turn it on.

Work happens one recording at a time and never starts while something is
recording. The original is only replaced once the smaller file checks out. If
BlammyTV closes partway, the original is kept and the job starts again at the
next launch. Deleting a recording ends its job. Install and Restart wait while
one runs.

## Pause and Rewind Live TV

Live TV keeps what you've watched on disk. You can pause a channel or rewind
it for two hours or more. It isn't recording. Change channel and the buffer is
gone. Press **Record** if you want to keep something.

- **Pause** with <kbd>Space</kbd> or the pause button.
- **Go back** with the **Back 10 Seconds** button or <kbd>J</kbd>. <kbd>←</kbd>
  goes back 5 seconds. Or drag along the bar above the controls. Its left end
  shows how far back you can go and its right end says **LIVE**.
- **Jump to Live**, the **LIVE** button, takes you back to the live edge. If
  the stream has kept up it just seeks. That's instant. If the stream stalled,
  it reloads the channel as it always did.

You can only go back as far as you've been watching that channel. The buffer
starts when you tune in.

The buffer lives in a `rewind-cache` folder in BlammyTV's data folder. It's
emptied at every launch. It stops growing at 12 GB or when the drive has under
2 GB free. The channel plays on. 12 GB is about two hours at 13 Mbit/s. A
channel with a higher bitrate reaches the cap sooner. The pop-out window gets
the same buffer. Movies and shows don't use it.
