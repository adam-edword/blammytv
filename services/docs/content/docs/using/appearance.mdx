title: Appearance
description: Themes, accent color, dark or light, where BlammyTV opens, the clock and how movies, shows and channels look.

You change how BlammyTV looks under **Settings → Appearance**. There's no Save
button. Each change applies as you make it.

The page has two groups. **Interface** is for the whole app, and **Media** is
for Stream and Live TV.

## Accent Color

The accent paints buttons, toggles and highlights. It's the first row under
**Interface**. There are nine swatches:

- **Default**, the first one, half light and half dark. It isn't a color. It
  follows Dark and Light, so buttons are light in Dark and dark in Light.
- **Red**, **Yellow**, **Green**, **Blue**, **Purple**, **Pink** and **Grey**.
- **Custom**, which opens a color picker.

In the picker, drag to a color, or type six hex digits into the field. A
color applies the moment the sixth digit is in. The eyedropper button, **Pick
from Screen**, picks any color you can see on your screen.

Custom remembers its last color. Pick a swatch, and Custom is one click away
when you want that color back. The text on buttons switches between black and
white so it stays readable on whatever you pick.

Some [themes](#theme) bring their own accent. See below.

## Dark, Light or Match Windows

The **Appearance** row sets the app's light or dark mode.

- **Dark** is the default.
- **Light** is the other one.
- **Match Windows** follows Windows. When you switch Windows between light and
  dark, BlammyTV switches with it, without a restart.

## Theme

**Theme** changes the colors of the whole app. There are four, shown as cards:

- **Default** is the app as it has always looked.
- **Prism** looks like the setup screens: true black, white type and faint
  dots behind everything. A soft light follows your mouse between the cards.
  The rainbow from the logo shows up in the Guide's now-line, the player's
  progress bar and the glow behind the Shelf carousel.
- **Velvet** is oxblood with a brass accent.
- **Lagoon** is deep teal with an aqua accent.

Themes are dark only. In Light, the app keeps its own look and the row says
**Themes are dark only.** Your pick comes back when you switch to Dark or when
**Match Windows** follows Windows back to dark.

Prism's light is off when your system asks for reduced motion. It also hides
while a player is up.

### Accent Under a Theme

- **Default** keeps the [accent picker](#accent-color) as it is.
- **Velvet** and **Lagoon** show the picker with the theme's own color as the
  first swatch, in place of Default. Pick any other swatch or a custom color.
  Each theme remembers its own pick. Velvet's doesn't change Lagoon's.
- **Prism** has no accent picker. Its rainbow is the accent.

## Startup Tab

**Startup Tab** is where BlammyTV opens: **Home**, **Live TV**, **Stream** or
**Stream · Discover**. The default is Live TV.

It only offers places you can use. Live TV needs a playlist. The others need
something to stream: AIOStreams, Jellyfin or an Xtream playlist with movies and
shows. See [Home](/using/home/). With no source at all the row isn't shown.

## Clock Format

**Clock Format** is **12h** or **24h**. It sets the clock at the top of the
window, and the times in the Guide, Sports and Multi-View. The default is 12h.

## Media

The **Media** group has two tabs, **Stream** and **Live TV**. It opens on
Stream.

### Stream

These settings show up once you have a source for movies and shows. Without
one, the page says to sign in to AIOStreams or Jellyfin, with a **Go to
Sources** button.

- **Featured Carousel** turns the sliding hero at the top of Stream on or off.
  Off takes it out completely, and the rows start at the top.
- **Carousel Style** is **Shelf** or **Large**. Shelf puts one title in focus
  with the next few as posters beside it. Large is one big card with **Watch
  Now** and **More Info** on it. It only shows while Featured Carousel is on.
- **Carousel Catalogs** picks which of your AIOStreams catalogs the carousel
  draws from. With none picked, it uses all of them. It only shows while
  Featured Carousel is on and you're signed in to AIOStreams.
- **Card Details** picks what shows under each title on a Stream card:
  **Rating**, **Year**, **Runtime**, **Genre** and **Type**. Runtime only
  appears when the catalog has it.
- **Info While Playing** picks what the player shows at the bottom left while a
  movie or show plays: **Logo Art**, **Season Number**, **Episode Number**,
  **Episode Title** and **Description**.
- **Titles per Row** sets how many titles each Stream row holds, from 10 to 100
  in steps of 5. The default is 40. Click the number to type an exact value.
  More titles take longer to load.

### Live TV

**Channel Numbers** shows your provider's channel number beside the channel
name at the top of the Guide. It's on by default.

## Reset Appearance

**Reset Appearance**, at the bottom of the page, puts the theme, accent color
(each theme's too), Dark or Light, clock format and channel numbers back to
their defaults. Press **Reset**, then **Click Again to Confirm**.

It leaves Startup Tab and the Stream settings as they are.
