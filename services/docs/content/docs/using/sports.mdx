---
title: Sports
description: Today's games, the channels showing them, and the leagues, teams and conferences you follow.
---

**Sports** lists today's games and matches each one to the channels in your
[playlists](/sources/). Click a game and it plays. The schedule and scores come
from ESPN, and the tab is marked **BETA** in the nav bar.

It needs a live TV playlist to have anything to play. Without one, the board
still lists games, with a note to add a playlist.

## Follow Leagues, Teams and Conferences

The sidebar on the left decides what the board shows. Follow nothing and you
get every league that has a game on, out of more than 150. Follow something
and the board narrows to it.

Follows are saved. They add up, so following the SEC and the Blackhawks shows
both. Collapse the sidebar with the button at its top, and open it again the
same way.

### Leagues

The **Leagues** tab lists every league, grouped by sport, with a search box at
the top.

- **Hover a league and click its star** to follow it. Your favorites move up to
  the top as tiles. To unfollow one, click the ✕ on its tile, then click again
  where it says **Remove from Favorites**.
- **Click a league or a tile** to see only that league. Pick as many as you
  like. This isn't saved, and it overrides your follows while it's on.
  **Clear Filter** drops all of your picks at once.

### Teams

The **Teams** tab lists the clubs in the games the board has loaded. Click one
to follow it. A club shows up once it has games, and an off-season league may
have none yet.

### Confs

**Confs**, short for conferences, only appears while the board has college
games on it. Click a conference to follow it.

- **Power 4** follows the ACC, Big 12, Big Ten and SEC in college football,
  plus the independents, in one click. Click it again to unfollow them.
- **Ranked** keeps only the college games with a top 25 team. Other games are
  left alone.

## Today's Games

The row at the top is today in kick-off order, with a wide card for each game.
It opens on what's live now, or the game that finished most recently if
nothing is, or the next one to start.

A card shows both teams, the score or the kickoff time, the league, and a line
along the bottom with the venue and where to watch:

- **Live on** or **On**, then a channel name, or a count like **3 channels**.
  These are channels from your playlists.
- **On** and a network, with a **Couldn't link** tag. ESPN named a network,
  but none of your channels match it.
- **Usually found on** a channel or network. This is a guess from where the
  league normally airs. The schedule didn't say.
- **Started** and a time, on a finished game.

Scores for today refresh about every 90 seconds.

## Days and Results

Under the row, each day gets a grid of small cards: **Today**, **Tomorrow**,
then a weekday and date. The board opens on today and tomorrow. Once you've
narrowed it to some leagues, teams or conferences, it opens on five days.

- **Show More Days** is under the last day. Each press loads more days.
- **Show Earlier Days** is above the board. Each press adds one day, up to
  three days back.
- **Compact Results**, on each day that has a finished game, shrinks every
  finished game to a small card with just the score, and puts those first.
  It's on by default.

## Watch a Game

Click a card. The game opens with the best channel already playing. A finished
game doesn't start on its own.

A game more than 16 hours from starting can't be opened yet. Its card does
nothing until then.

Beside the picture, **Channels** lists every channel of yours that may be
showing the game, best match first, with a count. Click one to switch to it.

- **Less Likely** holds the weaker matches, folded away. Each one shows its
  chance of being the game, as a percentage.
- **Right-click a channel** and choose **Wrong Channel for This Game** to mark
  it. The row says **Marked Wrong** while you're on that game.
- If a channel won't play, the player moves down the list to the next one by
  itself. When the list runs out, you get **Retry**.

**Live Scores**, further down, lists the other games on right now. Click one to
switch to it.

The player has the same controls and [keyboard
shortcuts](/using/live-tv/#keyboard-shortcuts) as Live TV. <kbd>Esc</kbd> and
<kbd>T</kbd> both step back, out of full screen first and then out of the
game. The **Back** button and your mouse's back button leave it too.

**Watch in Multi-View**, at the top right of the player, puts the game in your
[Multi-View](/using/multi-view/) grid as a game, so its tile shows the score.
The **Multi-View** button on the board takes you to the tab without adding
anything. It's missing when your line allows one stream at a time.

## Hide Finished

By the evening, most of Today's Games is over. **Hide Finished** takes
finished games out of that row and leaves the live and upcoming ones. It's off
by default, and it only shows up when something today has finished.

The day grids below still list every result. If everything today has finished,
the row says so and points you to them.

## Watch RedZone

When two or more NFL games are live at once and one of your channels is
RedZone, a **Watch RedZone** button shows up beside the board's other buttons.
It plays that channel in the Guide's player. Hover it to see which channel it
will use. It checks today's NFL games whatever you follow, so it shows up even
if you only follow a team that's off this week.

BlammyTV looks for a channel named RedZone on its own, and prefers one that
also says NFL. If it picks the wrong one, right-click the right channel in the
Guide and choose **Use for RedZone**. See
[Live TV](/using/live-tv/#right-click-a-channel). If the channel sits in a Guide
folder you've hidden, the button stays away.

Multi-View has the same thing as **Add RedZone** in its score row.

## When a Game Has No Channel

- With no live TV playlist, the board says to add one in **Settings →
  Sources**.
- If none of the games are on a channel in your playlist, one note at the top
  says so, instead of a tag on every card. Your provider doesn't carry the
  networks showing them.
- A single game with nothing matched shows **Couldn't link**. Opening it says
  which network has it, or that no broadcast is listed. There's nothing to
  play.

## Races, Golf and Tennis

Races, golf rounds and tennis tournaments are on the board too. Race and golf
cards are for reading: there's nothing to open. A tennis tournament opens its
draw, with a **Back to the Board** button. Nothing plays from the draw.
