Home

A Home of Your Own

Click the logo for one page of your rows in your order: Continue Watching, your catalogs, starred channels, today's games, recordings and saved lists.

  • Arrange, rename or switch off rows in Settings → Home.
  • Add Trakt's Trending and Popular lists.
  • Import a layout from Strand.
Live TV

Live TV with a Real Guide

Every channel you add lands in one guide. See what's on now and next, scroll a whole day ahead, and star the channels you watch most.

  • Hide the folders you never open.
  • Fix a channel's guide when its listings are wrong.
  • Add as many playlists as you like.
Record

Record Live TV

Record what's on or right-click a programme in the Guide to record it later. Pause live TV and rewind it for hours.

  • LIVE jumps back to now.
  • Recordings play from Library and Live TV.
  • Save recordings smaller to free up space.
Multi-View

Several Channels at Once

Right-click a channel in the Guide to add it. Watch them in a grid, or make one big and keep the rest beside it.

  • The sound follows the tile you pick.
  • Fill the grid with today's live games.
  • Live scores run along the bottom.
Sports

Find the Game

See today's games for the leagues you follow, with the channels showing them.

  • Follow leagues, teams and conferences.
  • Click a game to watch it on the channel showing it.
  • Hide finished games when you only want what's live.
Stream

Movies and Shows

Bring in your Xtream provider's movies and shows, sign in to AIOStreams, or add your own Jellyfin server. It all lands on the Stream page and Continue Watching keeps your place.

  • Browse your catalogs by genre, or see what's popular.
  • Keep what you've watched in sync with Trakt.
  • Track anime on MyAnimeList as you watch.
Player

Native Video Playback

Everything plays in mpv, built right in. Surround sound works, and Skip Intro jumps past the opening.

  • Pick your audio and subtitle languages once.
  • Tracks you pick for a show stay picked.
  • Up Next plays the next episode for you.

FAQ

Does it come with channels?

Nope. BlammyTV is just a player. It doesn't come with any channels, shows or streams. You add your own IPTV subscription, playlists, AIOStreams or Jellyfin server.

What can I add?

Xtream Codes logins, M3U playlists, Stalker portals, AIOStreams and your own Jellyfin server. Add as many as you like. An Xtream login brings its movies and shows too. Stalker portals haven't been tested much yet.

Can I record live TV?

Yup. Press Record while a channel plays or right-click a programme in the Guide. Recordings save to your PC and use one of your provider's streams while they run.

Do I need an account?

Nope. There's nothing to sign up for. Your sources stay on your PC, and any sign-ins are saved in Windows Credential Manager.
