FAQ

Is it really free?

Yup. There’s nothing to buy and nothing locked. Everything BlammyTV does is in the free download.

What content is provided?

None. BlammyTV is a media player only. It provides no channels, streams, or content of any kind. You bring your own IPTV subscription, playlists, or Stremio/AIOStreams setup, and BlammyTV plays them beautifully. Again, we provide no media content of any kind.

What sources are supported?

Currently Xtream Codes logins, M3U playlists (untested), Stalker portals (untested), and Stremio / AIOStreams manifests. Add as many as you like and BlammyTV brings them together in one app.

Accounts?

Nope. There's nothing to sign up for and no password to remember. Your sources are stored locally on your own device.

Does Sports need anything extra?

Nope, just a live TV playlist. Game schedules come from a free public source, and BlammyTV matches each game to the channels you already have.

Where did the themes go?

They’re coming back, with new looks. BlammyTV moved onto a new design and the old packs didn’t make the move. The accent picker did: pick your colour again in Settings → Customize.
