FAQ

Does it come with channels?

Nope. BlammyTV is just a player. It doesn't come with any channels, shows or streams. You add your own IPTV subscription, playlists, AIOStreams or Jellyfin server.

What can I add?

Xtream Codes logins, M3U playlists, Stalker portals, AIOStreams and your own Jellyfin server. Add as many as you like. Stalker portals haven't been tested much yet.

Do I need an account?

Nope. There's nothing to sign up for. Your sources stay on your PC, and any sign-ins are saved in Windows Credential Manager.
