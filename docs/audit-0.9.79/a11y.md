## BlammyTV a11y, keyboard and UX audit (v0.9.79, running app)

The biggest problems:
- The Guide's favourite star can't be seen at all, even after you star a channel.
- Focus rings measure 1.9:1 in dark and 1.5:1 in light. Four controls show no focus change at all.
- Settings doesn't move focus in, doesn't keep it in, and doesn't give it back. One Escape in the accent popover closes the whole sheet.
- Keyboard users land on off-screen hero buttons, and the Sports sidebar puts 305 tab stops ahead of the board.
- Light mode can't be switched on any more. When it is on (a stored preference from an older build), several on/off states disappear.

Everything below comes from the Vite dev server on :4301, driven with Playwright. Fake sources were served by in-process route interception, so no extra ports were used. axe-core 4.13 was installed in /tmp only. All evidence files are in `/tmp/audit-a11y/`. The repo is untouched, and my Vite on 4301 is killed. Another auditor's Vite on 4199 is still running; I left it alone.

Format: severity, CONFIRMED or SUSPECTED, what's wrong, where, evidence, fix.

---

### Global (tokens, focus, semantics)

1. **High, CONFIRMED. Focus rings are too faint to see.** `styles/ui.css:221-224` draws the ring as `--ring` at 50% (dark `tokens.css:158`, light `:310`). I compared pixels focused against unfocused: 1.82 to 1.89:1 in dark and 1.23 to 1.66:1 in light, on the nav, Settings button, guide cells, poster cards, chip tabs, swatches, toggles and the Reset button. Chip tabs are the worst at 1.32 dark and 1.23 light. Evidence: `sheet-focus-dark.png`, `focus-{dark,light}-*.png`. Fix: use `--ring` at full strength, or `--text` at 2px, so it reaches 3:1 against both the page and the surface.
2. **High, CONFIRMED. Some controls show no focus at all (0 pixels change).** These are the nav search input, the Catalog Row Size slider and the Guide star. Cause: `ui.css:226-231` removes `outline` from every `input`, and so does `base.css:770`. The range slider (`settings.css:829`) and the nav search input get nothing back. Fix: give `input[type=range]` and `.navcap__search:focus-within` a ring.
3. **Medium, CONFIRMED. Dim text fails contrast everywhere it's used.** `--text-dim` measures 4.17:1 in dark (`tokens.css:143`) and 2.58:1 in light (`:314`); it paints `.hero__synopsis` and `.hero__time`. `.settings__section-note--dim` uses opacity 0.4 (`settings.css:226-228`): 2.15 dark, 1.69 light, 6 or more per tab. The header clock uses opacity 0.3 and the version 0.35 (`base.css:355-370`): 2.53 and 1.88 dark, 2.04 and 1.57 light. `.playlist-row__source`: 2.63 dark, 2.04 light. Evidence: `survey-*.txt`, `axe-*.json`. Fix: drop the opacity tiers. Use `--text-muted`, and raise dark `--text-dim` to about oklch 0.6.
4. **Medium, CONFIRMED. The app has no `<h1>`, and heading levels skip.** Discover and Library start at H3. The Sports sidebar has H4s ahead of the board's H3. Detail has "More Like This" as H4 after "Sources" H3. Fix: one H1 per screen (it can be visually hidden), then H2 for sections.
5. **Low, CONFIRMED. There's no skip link.** It matters most on Sports (see SP2).
6. **Low, CONFIRMED. Target sizes are small.** Toggles are 32×18 (`ui/Toggle.tsx`), the Guide resize handle is 10px wide, and the nav search input is 19px tall.
7. **Low, SUSPECTED. Some live regions may not speak.** The `role="status"` and `role="alert"` regions are mounted together with their text (for example `LiveScreen.tsx:930-985`, `SportsScreen.tsx:803,851`). Many screen readers skip a region that arrives already filled. Fix: keep an empty region mounted and swap its text.

### Header and nav capsule

- **N1. Medium, CONFIRMED. The BETA chip is white-on-light in light mode.** It's hardcoded white (`rgba(255,255,255,.75)` on `.14`) at 8px (`base.css:386-400`) and measures 1.25:1. That also breaks the roadmap rule that new colour goes through a token. Evidence: `sheet-beta.png`. Fix: use tokens, and at least 10px.
- **N2. Low, CONFIRMED. The search placeholder is cut off** ("Search movies & serie…"). The text is 145px in a 145px field. Only the input takes a click: the magnifier and the pill padding (51 of 200px) are dead. Evidence: `discover-dark.png`. Fix: make the whole pill a `<label>`, or shorten the placeholder to "Search".
- **N3. Low, CONFIRMED. The Discover type chips use `aria-current="true"`** where `aria-pressed` fits (`AppHeader.tsx:675`).
- **N4. Low, CONFIRMED. The Settings button says "Settings" twice.** The Hint tooltip adds `aria-describedby` with the same word as the name (`AppHeader.tsx:754-760`).
- **N5. Low, CONFIRMED. Sizes and left edges don't line up.** The gear icon is 16px next to 23px nav icons. Left edges vary: clock x=17, sidebars x=12, Discover and Library content x=28.
- **N6. Low, CONFIRMED. The logo is a tab stop that only spins.** `navcap__mark`, named "BlammyTV".

### Live Guide (Guide tab)

- **LG1. High, CONFIRMED. The favourite star is invisible in every state.** `.guide__fav { opacity: 0 }` (`live.css:566-575`). The rule that revealed it (hover, focus-within, focus-visible, `--on`) only exists in the unloaded `styles/old/buttons-blammytv.css:270`; it moved there in v0.9.57 (67229a84). I measured opacity 0 at rest, on card hover, on star hover, and after starring (`aria-pressed=true`). Starring only shortens the channel name. The Favorites empty state (`LiveScreen.tsx:1032`) still says "Hover a channel card and hit the star". Evidence: `guide-after-fav.png`, `focus-dark-guide-fav.png`. Fix: put the reveal rule back in `live.css`.
- **LG2. Medium, CONFIRMED. There are no arrow keys; every programme is a tab stop.** Each row is 1 card, 1 star and N cells, and every cell does the same thing as the card (`onSelect(channel)`, `Guide.tsx:601-620`). ArrowUp, Down and Right do nothing. Fix: one tab stop for the grid, with arrows moving between cells (roving tabindex, the same pattern `RowScroller` already uses).
- **LG3. Low, CONFIRMED. The time ticks contain a literal "|".** `| {formatClock}` (`Guide.tsx:496`), so a screen reader says "vertical line 4:30 AM". Fix: draw the tick with a border.
- **LG4. Medium, CONFIRMED. Light-mode contrast in the Guide:** on-now `.guide__cell-time` is 3.39:1 at 10px, and `.guide-empty p` is 3.38:1. The 4K and FHD badges use 8.2px text.
- **LG5. Low, CONFIRMED. Copy mismatches.** "No Information" is Title Case (`Guide.tsx:598`) while its aria label reads "no programme information". The Recents empty state shows two different messages at once, one in the sidebar and one in the main area.
- **LG6. Low, CONFIRMED. The now-line draws over programme titles** for the first minutes of each slot. Evidence: `guide-light.png`.
- **LG7. Low, CONFIRMED. The sidebar tabs aren't tied to anything.** No `aria-controls`, no `tabpanel`, no label on the tablist. Arrow keys do work.
- **LG8. Low, CONFIRMED. A comment is out of date.** `live.css:963` says the hide eye is revealed on hover, but it's always shown. The eye-slash icon also reads like "this is hidden".

### Sports board

- **SP1. Medium, CONFIRMED. There's a 111px empty band above the board.** `.discover.sports` inherits `padding-top: 111px` from Discover's two-row nav. Multi-view and "Show earlier days" sit stacked below it. The sidebar top also jumps from 103 (Guide) to 115 (Sports). Evidence: `sports-dark.png`.
- **SP2. Medium, CONFIRMED. The sidebar has 305 tab stops before the first game** (first board control is tab #313; 151 leagues × "Show only" + "Add to favourites"). Fix: roving tabindex in the league list, or a skip link.
- **SP3. Low, CONFIRMED. League names show raw ESPN codes.** For example "NASCAR-PREMIER" is displayed when the catalog has "NASCAR Cup Series" (`leagues.json:707-708`). Also CBASE, CSOFT, WBBC, CMFS, "Conc CC", "Copa Mex". Fix: show `name`, keep `label` for tight spots.
- **SP4. Low, CONFIRMED. The same favourite control is spelled two ways.** Visible text is "Remove from Favorites" (`LeaguePicker.tsx:328`); its aria label is "…from favourites" (`:324`). The rest of Sports says "favourites" while Live says "favorites".
- **SP5. Low, CONFIRMED. Two toggles in one row look different.** "Hide finished" is an outlined 11px pill; "Compact results" is filled. Both use `aria-pressed`. Off, "Hide finished" looks disabled.
- **SP6. Low, CONFIRMED. Light-mode game card text is 4.22:1** (`.gamecard__abbr` and `.gamecard__record` at 12px, and `.compactcard__abbr`).
- **SP7. Low, CONFIRMED. The row's right chevron sits on top of the next card's score.** Evidence: `sports-sidebar-collapsed-dark.png`.

### Multi-view (Sports → Multi-view)

- **MV1. Medium, CONFIRMED. The notice is marked modal but doesn't trap focus.** It's `aria-modal` (`MultiviewNotice.tsx:43`) with `autoFocus` on "Got it" (`:66`), but Tab leaves it for the header and the game rail. There's no scrim, and everything behind stays clickable.
- **MV2. Medium, CONFIRMED. The layout breaks at 1400×900.** The tiles squeeze to 115px, the header clock is drawn over tile 1, and the capsule covers "0/2". Away team names are cut mid-word ("Riv / Ott"), and tile labels truncate ("Nothing here…"). Evidence: `multiview-dark.png`.
- **MV3. Low, CONFIRMED. "Plays in your browser, not mpv"** means nothing to users.

### Stream home

- **ST1. High, CONFIRMED. Keyboard focus goes to buttons you can't see.** The Featured carousel has 12 tab stops, and 8 of them are Watch Now or More Info on off-screen slides (x = -1754, -744, 2286, 3296). Each slide is a `role="button"` containing buttons (axe nested-interactive ×6, `StreamScreen.tsx:2250-2262`).
- **ST2. Medium, CONFIRMED. Autoplay takes focus away.** The carousel advances every 8s and pauses only on mouse hover (`StreamScreen.tsx:2165-2168`). With focus on Watch Now, 9s later that button sits at x=-744, off screen. It keeps auto-advancing under reduced motion, there's no pause control, and ArrowRight does nothing. Evidence: `stream-hero-focus-after-advance.png`. Fix: pause on focus-within and under reduced motion, and make inactive slides `inert`.
- **ST3. Low, CONFIRMED. The fallback letter ends up in the poster's name** ("F Fake Movie Seven").
- **ST4. Low, CONFIRMED. Button casing doesn't match the rest of the app:** "Watch Now" and "More Info" versus "Show earlier days" and "Compact results".

### Title detail (Stream → a card)

- **DT1. Medium, CONFIRMED. The cached marker is only an emoji.** `⚡` (`StreamScreen.tsx:1494, 2818`) is read as "high voltage". Fix: `aria-label="Instant"`, or visually-hidden text.
- **DT2. Low, CONFIRMED. The Back button's name includes the arrow glyph** ("← Back"; `LibraryScreen.tsx:257`, `StreamScreen.tsx:2753, 2953`, `Onboarding.tsx:859`). Fix: an icon plus "Back".
- **DT3. Low, CONFIRMED. The layout is uneven.** Back sits at x=48 while content starts at x=90. Vertical gaps run 100/63/53px. "More Like This" is 14px grey while "Sources" is 20px. Source focus rings are clipped by `.vod-sources`. Evidence: `detail-movie-dark.png`.

### Discover, search, REC

That's a dead end, and not the house voice. The input's name is "Add a keyword" while its placeholder says "What do you want to watch?". Evidence: `rec-dark.png`.
- **DI2. Medium, CONFIRMED. The no-source empty states are dead ends.** Stream and Discover say "Settings → General → Sources" but have no button (0 buttons in main). ### Library

- **LB1. Low, CONFIRMED. The new-list field is named only by its placeholder** ("List name").
- **LB2. Low, CONFIRMED. The "Click again to confirm" buttons are silent and mouse-worded.** Delete, Clear history and Settings → Clear… change their text, which isn't announced, the wording assumes a mouse, and Clear… disarms after 4s. Settings → Customize → Reset has no confirm at all.

### Settings (sheet)

- **SE1. High, CONFIRMED. Focus isn't managed.** Opened with Enter, focus stays on the header gear, and the next Tab goes to the Stream hero behind the sheet. There's no `aria-modal` (`SettingsModal.tsx:81-85`) and no focus trap: after Reset, Tab walks the entire app. Closing sends focus to BODY instead of the gear. The page behind isn't `inert`, and there's no scrim.
- **SE2. High, CONFIRMED. Escape inside the accent popover closes both the popover and Settings.** The cause is the window keydown listener at `SettingsModal.tsx:64-72`. Focus ends on BODY. Fix: Base UI Dialog, as planned in M2.
- **SE3. High (light mode), CONFIRMED. On and off look the same in light mode.** Card Details and Player Overlay chips use `secondary` for on (`CustomizeTab.tsx:355`), which is `--surface-raised: oklch(1 0 0)` (`tokens.css:294`). Chip against sheet measures 1.0:1. Evidence: `sheet-toggles.png`.
- **SE4. Medium, CONFIRMED. Light mode can't be turned on.** Nothing in the UI sets it. The comment at `CustomizeTab.tsx:115-116` points to a Themes panel that is parked. Yet Reset says "Accent, theme, scale…" (`:592`), Default says "follows light and dark", and onboarding mentions "themes".
- **SE5. Critical per axe, CONFIRMED. Two icon buttons have no name** in Customize → Preferred Language: the combobox chevron and clear buttons (`components/ui/combobox.tsx:42-52, 74-83`).
- **SE6. Low, CONFIRMED. The Settings rails are toggle buttons, not tabs.** ChipTabs uses `aria-pressed`, with no tablist, tabpanel or arrow keys (`ui/ChipTabs.tsx`). Inactive chip text in light is 3.62:1 at 12px.
- **SE7. Low, CONFIRMED. The same Live TV / Stream pill is in a different order on each tab.** General has Live TV first (`GeneralTab.tsx:24-26`); Customize has Stream first (`CustomizeTab.tsx:82-83`).
- **SE8. Low, CONFIRMED. Placeholders look like filled-in values.** "user123" and "••••••••" in the Xtream form. Evidence: `settings-general-dark.png`.
- **SE9. Low, CONFIRMED. Casing and spelling drift.** Headings are Title Case ("Featured Carousel", "One-Click Play Movies", "Clear All Login Info") while the matching switch names are sentence case ("Featured carousel", "One-click play"). Spelling mixes "Customize" with "Accent colour", "Hex colour", "Grey", and react-colorful's own "Color" and "Hue" in the same popover.
- **SE10. Low, CONFIRMED. AIOStreams feedback isn't announced.** Connection Test results have no live region, and "Copied!" only changes the `title` (`AioStreamsTab.tsx:79, 118-140`).
- **SE11. Low, CONFIRMED. The sheet covers part of the nav.** On Discover it hides the capsule's second row (search and REC) at 80%, 100% and 120%. At 120% on 1280×720 it runs off the bottom of the window. Evidence: `scale-s1.2-1280x720-settings.png`, `scale-s0.8-1400x900-settings.png`. The body also overflows sideways by 3px.

### Accent extremes (Settings → Customize → Custom hex)

- **AC1. Medium, CONFIRMED. The text-colour flip works; the rest doesn't.** Button text stayed at or above 4.54:1 in every case I tried. But at light + #f5f5f5, an on toggle measures 1.09:1 against the sheet and 1.1:1 against an off toggle, and the primary button is 1.09:1 against the sheet. At dark + #141414, the primary button is 1.03:1 against the sheet and the on-toggle track disappears. At light + #ffd500, the toggle track is 1.42:1. Evidence: `sheet-toggles.png`, `accent-*-replay.png`. Fix: clamp accent lightness per theme, or add a border when the accent is within 1.5:1 of `--surface`.

### Onboarding (first run)

- **OB1. Low, CONFIRMED. Copy is out of date.** "Home · Discover · My List" when the nav says Library (`Onboarding.tsx:805`). "A couple of preferences" when only the clock is left (`:746`). "…sources, themes, playback…" when there are no themes (`:810-811`). "Get Started" next to "Skip setup" (`:531` vs `:864`).

### Motion and UI scale

- **MO1. Low, CONFIRMED. The Discover second row still animates under reduced motion.** `.navcap__row` keeps a 380ms height and transform transition because it's missing from the block at `base.css:1106-1114`. `chip-tabs__tab` (160ms transform) and the poster hover lift also keep theirs. `base.css:371` is an empty reduced-motion block.
- **MO2. Low, SUSPECTED.** `REDUCED_MOTION` is read once at load (`lib/reducedMotion.ts`), so changing the OS setting while the app is open does nothing.
- **MO3. Low, CONFIRMED. Nothing overflows the page at 80% or 120%**, apart from SE11 and MV2. At 80%, the 8px BETA text renders at 6.4px.

### Five cheap fixes with the biggest payoff

1. Put the Guide star's reveal rule back into `live.css` (one rule). Starring in the Guide becomes visible again.
2. Make the ring solid: change `ui.css:222` to use `var(--ring)` at 100% and add a range-input and `:focus-within` ring. This fixes the 1.9:1 and 0:1 findings across the whole app.
3. Settings on Base UI Dialog, or at minimum: move focus in, trap it, give it back, and skip Escape when the event is already handled. That fixes SE1 and SE2 and later helps MV1.
4. In light mode, set `--surface-raised` to oklch 0.97, and remove the opacity-based dim text (clock, version, `--dim` notes). That clears SE3 and most contrast failures.
5. Stream hero: mark non-active slides `inert` and pause autoplay on focus and under reduced motion. That removes 8 invisible tab stops and stops autoplay moving focused buttons off screen.

### What I couldn't check

- Anything in the native mpv overlay: theater, player shortcuts, and how you get from a selected channel to theater by keyboard.
- Real posters under the hero text.
- WebView2's own focus behaviour.
- A real TMDB key or a real playlist.
- Sports tab-stop counts use the real 151-league catalog but fake games. With 450 fake games, the light-mode Sports board sometimes took 20s or more to answer a screenshot. I didn't chase that, so treat it as unproven.
